Draft, not bench-tested. All sensors use the external 5 V rail; only the level-shifter LV reference connects to MKR 3V3. Common ground is essential. Never power sensors from GPIO. I²C uses parallel SDA/SCL connections, not a serial chain.

The analog sensor connects through its own resistor divider, not through the I²C shifter. Verify divider output remains below 3.3 V. Divider loading and different ADC scaling require baseline/threshold validation.

If using two HM3301 modules, add an I²C multiplexer and put each HM3301 on a separate channel. Do not simply parallel their identical addresses.

Power up the board and sensor rails together; do not leave live bus signals driving an unpowered board. Confirm total operating/start-up current before choosing a final supply.
